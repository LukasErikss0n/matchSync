# Pricing - MatchCalender

MatchCalender is free. There is one tier, it costs nothing, and there is no paid
upgrade, trial, or usage cap to exceed.

## Free
- Price: $0
- Billing: none, no payment method collected
- Account required: no
- Limits: none published
- Included:
  - Unlimited calendar subscription links
  - All covered leagues (football, hockey, basketball, motorsport)
  - Team-level and league-level feeds
  - Automatic sync of reschedules and playoff rounds
  - Standard iCal (.ics) output, works with Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Outlook
  - No ads, no tracking, no account

## Restrictions
Use is personal. Sharing a calendar link with friends is allowed. Reselling,
sublicensing, or bundling the data into a paid or commercial offering is not.
See https://matchcalender.com/terms
